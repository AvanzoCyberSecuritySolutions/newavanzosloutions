FAQ

How we work, and what to expect.

Can't find your answer? Contact our team.

What happens in a consultation?

You tell us about your issue or concern, and we connect you with the right experts: our founder, or our legal, technical or psychology teams. Consultations are available at our Thrissur and Mangalore offices.

Can you help if we've already been attacked?

Yes. We help contain the incident, recover your systems and investigate what happened, including the legal side of cybercrime cases.

Do you help with compliance and data privacy?

Yes. Our compliance team helps you understand your obligations under data protection regulations and puts practical controls in place to meet them.

Which product is right for my business?

Cyber Hulk is a free app for individuals and awareness. MyCISO protects your endpoints. CyberSol is our complete package for organisations. Book a consultation and we'll recommend the right fit.

Is Cyber Hulk really free?

Yes. Cyber Hulk is free to download and includes threat alerts, a password strength checker, a network scanner and security guides.
